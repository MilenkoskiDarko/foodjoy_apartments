About Us

Situated in a quiet area, the apartments provide the perfect balance between central convenience and peaceful surroundings. The property is also ideally positioned just 700m from city centre,1km from the lake.
Each apartment features a generous and functional layout, a fully equipped kitchen, well-maintained bathroom. Guests benefit from high-speed WiFi and free private parking throughout their stay.
Foodjoy Apartments are part of the Foodjoy Bakery, located in the same building and known for its artisan sourdough bread. Guests can enjoy freshly baked sourdough specialties from our bakery, adding a unique and welcoming touch to their stay.
